LEARNING ARCHITECTURE

Why your argument was challenged.

ARIA exposes structural weaknesses in your reasoning so you can systematically inspect and improve your arguments.

ARIA detects unverified premises treated as absolute facts, prompting you to justify foundational beliefs before building further conclusions.

Identifies generalized claims lacking verifiable citations, empirical metrics, or concrete data points to support core assertions.

Highlights reasoning where correlation is incorrectly interpreted as direct causation without a valid bridging mechanism.

Exposes competing variables and alternative explanations that could account for the same phenomenon.
